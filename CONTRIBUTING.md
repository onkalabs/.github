# ONKA repository contribution policy

## Documents

All new internal and external ONKA documents must follow the [ONKA Official Document Design System](https://github.com/onka-inc/brand/blob/main/guidelines/onka-document-production-standard.md).

This includes agreements, policies, payment terms, resolutions, notices, reports, registers, briefs, proposals, guides, handbooks, and presentation-style PDFs.

Invoices, quotes, and receipts are the only exceptions. Historical issued, signed, or filed records must not be restyled.

Use Avenir Next, the official ONKA lockup, the black left rail, the approved lifecycle language, and the release checks in the standard. Do not introduce a competing document template or revive the retired heavy-rule shell.
